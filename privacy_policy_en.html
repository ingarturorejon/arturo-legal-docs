Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Silice Games by Arturo González Rejón ("we", "our", or "the developer") collects, uses, and protects information when you use any mobile application developed and published by Silice Games by Arturo González Rejón on Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, or using our applications, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

Our applications may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • Unique device identifiers (for advertising)
  • App usage data (play time, features used)
  • Crash and technical error information
  • IP address (anonymized)

1.2 Information We DO NOT Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as:

  • Real name
  • Email address (unless you contact us directly)
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Financial or credit card information (payments are processed directly by Google Play)

2. Use of Information

The collected information is used to:

  • Provide and improve our applications
  • Personalize user experience
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Analyze app performance and usage
  • Detect and prevent technical issues
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

Our applications use third-party services that may collect information. These services include:

3.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use data to show personalized ads. You can review Google's privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Google Play Services

Our applications use Google Play Services for features like in-app purchases and billing. You can review the terms at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 Firebase (Google)

Some of our applications use Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication (for Google Sign-In) and Firestore (for cloud save synchronization). Firebase may collect usage and diagnostic data. You can review Firebase's privacy information at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Advertising

Some of our applications display ads provided by Google AdMob. These ads may be:

  • Personalized ads: Based on your interests and activity

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed directly by Google Play. We do not have access to your financial information. To manage your purchases, visit your Google Play account settings.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect collected information. However, no method of Internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

8. Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.
